🎓 SLIIT Courseweb Cleaner

A context-aware browser extension and userscript designed to clean up your SLIIT Moodle interface with automated batch/campus filtering and high-contrast dark theme.

Add to Chrome / Brave Get for Microsoft Edge Get for Mozilla Firefox Install Userscript (.user.js)

✨ Key Features (v7.1.0)

  • 🌙
    Dynamic Dark Theme: High-contrast, eye-friendly theme powered by Dark Reader across all Courseweb pages.
  • 🧭
    Toolbar Action Popup: Switch profiles, toggle dark mode, or adjust ghost mode directly from your browser toolbar.
  • 🎯
    Campus & Batch Filtering: Keeps Malabe, Metro, Kandy, Kurunegala, Matara, and Jaffna modules organized.
  • 👻
    Ghost Mode: Optionally dims unassigned items to 25% opacity rather than hiding them completely.
  • 🌐
    Partner Program Isolation: Distinguishes external partner degree materials (Deakin, Curtin) cleanly.
  • 🛡️
    Notice & Form Protection: Critical notices, reschedule announcements, and Moodle forms are never hidden.
  • ⚡
    Custom Whitelist & Blacklist: Pin priority assignments or mute specific lecturers with keywords.
  • 🔄
    Cloud Sync & Community Rules: Syncs across your signed-in browsers and updates regex patterns in the background.
🤖 Ecosystem Spotlight: Explore the SLIIT Courseweb MCP Server by Kesaru Hasun to connect AI assistants (Claude, Cursor, etc.) directly with your Courseweb materials and notices.
